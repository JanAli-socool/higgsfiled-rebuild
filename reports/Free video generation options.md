# Trial quotas, not free tiers, power demo video

As of October 2026 there is **no free, no-card video API that can serve real traffic**. What exists is a set of **one-time trial quotas and tiny daily GPU allowances, enough for roughly 10–40 five-second clips in total across all of them**. The best primary option for a Next.js `ModelProvider` adapter is **Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (International/Singapore)**. It gives about **50 free seconds per Wan model, including wan2.7-i2v at 720p**, needs only mobile verification and no card, and its API is already a native submit → poll → fetch flow. The best second option is a **Hugging Face ZeroGPU Space running Wan 2.2 14B image-to-video**, called with `@gradio/client`. The quality is good, but one server token gets only **5 GPU-minutes per day for the whole app, about 4 clips**. Google, Cloudflare and HF Inference Providers all offer **no free video at all**. Self-hosting on Kaggle or Colab works only in a technical sense: a free T4 takes minutes to tens of minutes per clip, and Colab's terms explicitly forbid it. Quotas also sit at the wrong level for a shared-server app. Every free allowance is **per account or per token, not per end user**, so the app must ration clips itself (a global counter plus a per-user cap) and fail over through a chain of providers. For a 24-hour take-home, integrate Alibaba first, HF Spaces second, small credits from WaveSpeed or SiliconFlow third, and a clearly labeled pre-rendered clip as the last fallback.

## Three big names are dead ends for free video

**Google no longer offers free video through the Gemini API.** The docs state that "The Veo 3.1 Preview in the Gemini API has been shut down" ([Gemini API video docs](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/video)). Its replacement, Gemini Omni Flash (`gemini-omni-1.1-flash`), is listed as **"Not available" on the free tier** and costs about **$0.10 per second of 720p video**, or about $0.50 per 5 s clip, on the paid tier ([Gemini API pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing)). The $300 Google Cloud trial needs a card. For accounts opened after 2 March 2026 it also no longer pays for Gemini API or AI Studio usage ([klymentiev.com](https://klymentiev.com/blog/gemini-free-credits)). Vertex AI express mode needs no billing details, but nothing found shows that it includes Veo or Omni ([klymentiev.com](https://klymentiev.com/blog/gemini-free-credits)). Blog posts that promise "Veo on the free tier" are out of date.

**Cloudflare Workers AI has no video generation model in its catalog.** Its task types stop at text-to-image, speech and classification ([Workers AI models](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/models/)). Its 10,000 free Neurons a day are only useful for making a starting frame that is then sent to a video API ([Workers AI pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/pricing/)).

**Hugging Face Inference Providers now give free accounts no monthly credits.** The pricing page reads "Free Users: None … (credits purchase required)", while PRO gets $2 a month ([HF Inference Providers pricing](https://huggingface.co/docs/inference-providers/pricing)). The router does serve Wan 2.2 I2V, LTX-2 and HunyuanVideo-I2V through fal and WaveSpeed ([HF models API](https://huggingface.co/api/models?pipeline_tag=image-to-video&inference_provider=all&expand[]=inferenceProviderMapping)). But Wan 2.2 image-to-video on fal costs **$0.04–$0.08 per video second** ([fal model page](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/wan/v2.2-a14b/image-to-video)), so a free account cannot make a single clip. fal's own "five free FLUX 3 Max videos a day" offer runs only in its sandbox, not the API, and "Activating an account takes a card on file" ([fal FLUX video free](https://fal.ai/flux-video-free)).

## Alibaba's Wan quota is the only no-card hosted API that fits the adapter

**Alibaba Cloud Model Studio gives new International accounts a 90-day free quota for each model.** The image-to-video entries are **wan2.7-i2v and its dated snapshot wan2.7-i2v-2026-04-25, each with 50 s free at 720p with audio** (list price $0.10/s). **wan3.0-video and wan3.0-video-prime each add 30 s** at 480p, and the text-to-video models carry 50 s each ([Model Studio pricing](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/model-pricing)). Activation requires "mobile security verification", not a card. The quota applies only to the Singapore region with International deployment scope ([Model Studio free quota](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/new-free-quota)). That is **about 10 five-second 720p I2V clips per model ID**. Because the pricing table lists the quota per model ID, the snapshot and Wan 3.0 entries probably add more, for **an estimated 20–25 image-to-video clips in total**. That total is an inference from the table layout; Alibaba does not state it explicitly.

The trap is billing. **Once the quota is used up, the account is auto-billed pay-as-you-go unless "Free Quota Only" is switched on, and it is off by default.** With it on, requests fail with `AllocationQuota.FreeTierOnly` ([Model Studio free quota](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/new-free-quota)). That error code doubles as a clean signal for the adapter to fail over to the next provider. Failed calls do not use up quota ([Wan i2v guide](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/wan-image-to-video-guide)).

The API shape fits the existing adapter almost exactly. You POST to the video-synthesis endpoint with `X-DashScope-Async: enable` and get back a `task_id`. You then poll `GET /api/v1/tasks/{task_id}` and download the result URL, which **expires after 24 hours**, so copy it to Supabase storage right away. Clips can run 2–15 s at 720p or 1080p. The international base URL is `https://{WorkspaceId}.ap-southeast-1.maas.aliyuncs.com/api/v1`. The examples show a settable `watermark` parameter ([Wan i2v guide](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/wan-image-to-video-guide)). Some things remain unknown: the real latency (no primary figure was found), whether the verification phone number must come from a particular country, and what the terms say specifically about a public multi-user app.

**The other no-card credits buy a handful of clips each.** WaveSpeed gives eligible new accounts **$1 with "no credit card required"**, though "some premium models may not be available with trial credits". Its 5 s clip prices start at $0.20 for MiniMax H3 and $0.25 for Wan 3.0, so $1 buys about 4–5 clips ([WaveSpeed pricing](https://wavespeed.ai/pricing)). SiliconFlow offers **$1 in free credits** against **$0.29 per Wan2.2-I2V-A14B video**, about 3 clips ([SiliconFlow pricing](https://www.siliconflow.com/pricing)). Novita's $0.50 signup credit is reported only by a secondary source ([OmniRoute issue](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/issues/6658)). **Pollinations is the open question.** It has a key-authenticated `GET /video/{prompt}` endpoint that accepts a start frame through an `image` array and serves Wan, Seedance, Kling, Veo 3.1 Fast and others. However, unbound publishable keys are capped at "1 pollen / IP / hour", and video can cost up to 0.5 Pollen per second ([Pollinations API docs](https://gen.pollinations.ai/docs)). Whether a free key can make even one 5 s clip is unconfirmed. Its synchronous design (the call returns the MP4) also sits awkwardly within Vercel's 300 s function limit ([Vercel limits](https://vercel.com/docs/functions/limitations)).

## ZeroGPU Spaces give the best free quality, but only about four clips a day per token

**Hugging Face Spaces are where free, high-quality Wan 2.2 actually runs today.** On 27 October 2026 the HF API showed **`zerogpu-aoti/wan2-2-fp8da-aoti-faster`** (Wan 2.2 14B I2V, FP8 with Lightning, 3,774 likes) as RUNNING on ZeroGPU, along with the `r3gm/wan2-2-fp8da-aoti-preview*` forks and Lightricks' official **`Lightricks/LTX-2-3`** and `LTX-2.5`. Meanwhile, the official `Wan-AI/Wan2.1`, `Lightricks/ltx-2` and most FramePack and HunyuanVideo Spaces were in RUNTIME_ERROR or gone ([HF Spaces API](https://huggingface.co/api/spaces?search=wan&expand[]=runtime)). The Wan Space's `/generate_video` endpoint takes an image and a prompt and outputs **up to 5.0 s at 16 fps, 832×480**, with a default of 3.5 s ([app.py](https://huggingface.co/spaces/zerogpu-aoti/wan2-2-fp8da-aoti-faster/raw/main/app.py)). LTX-2-3 produces 1–10 s clips at up to 1536×1024 but reserves a fixed 75 s of GPU per call ([app.py](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Lightricks/LTX-2-3/raw/main/app.py)).

**The quota sets the ceiling.** Current daily ZeroGPU allowances are **2 minutes unauthenticated, 5 minutes for a free account and 40 for PRO**, resetting 24 h after first use. **xlarge Spaces cost 2× quota** and queue longer ([HF ZeroGPU docs](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/spaces-zerogpu)). The quota is checked against the *declared* GPU duration, not the time actually used ("60s requested vs. 0s left") ([HF Forum](https://discuss.huggingface.co/t/it-seems-use-60-sec-gpu-quota-instead-of-real-time-usage/175130)). ZeroGPU decides whose quota to charge from the `X-IP-Token` header or the caller's HF token ([Gradio docs](https://www.gradio.app/docs/python-client/using-zero-gpu-spaces); [gradio#13966](https://github.com/gradio-app/gradio/issues/13966)). A Vercel backend outside HF cannot obtain each visitor's `X-IP-Token`. The result is that **every user of the app shares one free account's 300 s a day**. The Space's own formula puts a 5 s 832×480 clip at about 70 s of requested GPU time, which works out to **roughly 4 clips per day per token**, or about 1 if the call is anonymous. These figures are derived from the code, not measured. The only legitimate way to give each user their own allowance is for each user to sign in with HF and have the backend pass their token. Rotating IPs or tokens to dodge quotas falls under "Abuse or interference with Hugging Face services" ([HF Content Policy](https://huggingface.co/content-policy)).

Integration takes more work than with Alibaba. `@gradio/client` (`Client.connect("owner/space", { token })`, then `submit()` with `handle_file(buffer)`) streams queue position and ETA over SSE ([Gradio JS client guide](https://www.gradio.app/guides/getting-started-with-the-js-client)). It is not confirmed that results can be fetched again after the connection drops, so the "poll" step must hold that stream open. That fits inside Vercel Hobby's **300 s** function limit only when the queue is short ([Vercel limits](https://vercel.com/docs/functions/limitations)). The returned file lives in the Space's temporary cache and must be copied right away. Reliability is the other weak point. Endpoint signatures differ between forks and change with updates, and quota and "GPU task aborted" errors are recurring forum topics ([HF Forum 177659](https://discuss.huggingface.co/t/zero-gpu-limit-reached-even-through-failed-attempts/177659)). The adapter should pin a specific Space, check `view_api()` at startup, and treat a quota error as a failover signal. You can duplicate a Space to your own free account to protect against upstream changes, but only if the account is **more than 30 days old with a verified email**, and the copy still draws on callers' quotas ([HF ZeroGPU docs](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/spaces-zerogpu)).

## Self-hosting on free GPUs is too slow or against the rules for a live demo

The open models line up clearly on quality and cost. **Wan 2.2** gives the best open quality and is trained on roughly 5 s clips. **LTX distilled** is much faster but "often not as good as WAN" ([Reddit r/StableDiffusion](https://www.reddit.com/r/StableDiffusion/comments/1j5k9k5/ltxv_vs_wan21_vs_hunyuan_insane_speed_differences/)). **HunyuanVideo** looks strong but needs 60–80 GB unoptimized, and its license "DOES NOT APPLY IN THE EUROPEAN UNION, UNITED KINGDOM AND SOUTH KOREA" ([HunyuanVideo-1.5 LICENSE](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanVideo-1.5/blob/main/LICENSE); [Spheron](https://www.spheron.network/blog/ai-video-generation-gpu-guide/)). **CogVideoX-5B** is a 2024 model with a registration-gated commercial license ([ThakiCloud audit](https://thakicloud.com/tech-blog/en/llmops/open-video-model-license-territory-audit/)). **FramePack** runs at 2.5 s per frame on a 4090 ([FramePack](https://lllyasviel.github.io/frame_pack_gitpage/)), which puts a 5 s clip at many minutes even on fast hardware. LTX-2 needs a paid license above $10M in revenue ([Turing Post](https://www.turingpost.com/p/6-opensource-video-generation-models)). The only realistic model for a 16 GB T4 is **Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B**, which takes "under 9 minutes" for 5 s at 720p on a 4090 ([HF model card](https://huggingface.co/Wan-AI/Wan2.2-TI2V-5B)). On a T4, that extrapolates to **roughly 10–30 minutes per clip, plus 5–15 minutes of cold start** to re-download 20–35 GB of weights each session. No first-hand T4 benchmark exists, so this estimate is uncertain, but it is clearly not demo-friendly.

The free compute platforms each fail for a different reason. **Colab free** explicitly forbids "connecting to remote proxies" and "web service offerings", and warns that free-tier users "commonly experience runtime terminations" when serving a web UI for content generation ([Colab FAQ](https://research.google.com/colaboratory/faq.html)). **Kaggle** gives T4×2 for about 30 h a week with 12 h sessions ([Kaggle docs](https://www.kaggle.com/docs/notebooks); [Clusy](https://www.clusy.io/compare/colab-vs-kaggle)). People do run FastAPI behind ngrok or cloudflared tunnels there ([GitHub example](https://github.com/kumaresankp/kaggle_32gpu_free)), but part of the community calls that abuse and has asked Kaggle to block tunnels ([Kaggle discussion 743898](https://www.kaggle.com/discussions/product-feedback/743898)). The tunnel URL also changes every session. **Lightning AI** advertises "80 free GPU hours", but without a card you get only **5 credits** (about 9 T4-hours), and free Studios restart every 4 hours ([Lightning pricing](https://lightning.ai/pricing)). **Modal Starter** offers **$30 a month** with a proper serverless endpoint and 10-GPU concurrency ([Modal pricing](https://modal.com/pricing)), which would make it the most production-like option. However, secondary sources contradict each other on whether a card is required, and this could not be confirmed from modal.com ([UsagePricing](https://www.usagepricing.com/blueprint/modal); [aicreditmart](https://aicreditmart.com/ai-credits-providers/modal-free-tier-how-to-get-30-month-in-compute-credits-2026/)).

## Integrate Alibaba first, then chain to Hugging Face

For a 24-hour take-home, the ranking follows from three things: how well each option fits the existing adapter, how much free capacity it has, and how likely it is to work on the day of review.

**1. Alibaba Model Studio `wan2.7-i2v` (primary).** This is the only no-card option with a true submit → poll → fetch API, 720p image-to-video, and a quota (about 10 clips per model ID, more across model IDs) big enough for development and a reviewer session. Turn on **Free Quota Only before writing any code**, set `watermark` as you want it, and copy outputs to Supabase within 24 h. Map `AllocationQuota.FreeTierOnly` to a "provider exhausted" error that triggers failover. Rotate model IDs (`wan2.7-i2v` → `wan2.7-i2v-2026-04-25` → `wan3.0-video`) to stretch the quota.

**2. HF ZeroGPU `zerogpu-aoti/wan2-2-fp8da-aoti-faster` (secondary).** This option has the best confirmed free quality, and it resets daily. Call it with the server's HF token through `@gradio/client`, at 3.5–5 s and 832×480. If it hits a quota error or a RUNTIME_ERROR, fall through to **`Lightricks/LTX-2-3`**. As an optional upgrade, add "Sign in with Hugging Face" so each reviewer spends their own 5 minutes instead of the app's.

**3. WaveSpeed and SiliconFlow $1 credits (tertiary).** Both use the same async shape for a few more clips at no cost, and they are useful as a third and fourth link if signup goes smoothly.

**4. Pre-rendered showcase clip (terminal fallback).** Generate a few clips in advance with the providers above. When every live provider is exhausted, return one of these, **clearly labeled as cached**, so the demo never shows a dead end.

The resulting chain is **Alibaba wan2.7-i2v → Alibaba wan3.0-video → HF Wan 2.2 Space → HF LTX-2-3 Space → WaveSpeed/SiliconFlow → labeled cached clip**. Pollinations should join the chain only after a manual test confirms that a free key can make a 5 s clip. Kaggle should be added only if there is time left over, as an "own GPU" proof point rather than a dependency. Two app-level guards matter more than which provider is chosen. First, keep a **global daily clip budget and a per-user cap** (for example 1–2 clips per user), because every free quota is per account, not per end user. Second, run every job as a **database-backed background job** with status polling from the frontend, since HF streams and possible queue waits can exceed Vercel's 300 s limit.

## Conclusion

"Free video generation" in October 2026 means stitching together trial credits, not choosing a free tier. The engineering that makes the demo credible is therefore the fallback chain, the quota accounting and the cached terminal fallback, rather than any single integration. For the take-home, these are worth explaining to the reviewer as deliberate design. The provider-agnostic `ModelProvider` adapter is the right shape for this, because it allows a paid key (Alibaba pay-as-you-go at $0.10/s, or fal Wan at $0.04–0.08/s) to replace the trial quotas with a config change once the app has real users.

Several uncertainties remain to resolve in the first hour of building: Alibaba's real latency and phone-country rules, whether Pollinations' free key covers video, whether Modal requires a card, and whether a Gradio result survives a dropped connection. Each can be settled by one test call, and the ranking above changes only if Alibaba's signup fails. In that case the HF Space becomes the primary, and the per-user HF sign-in stops being optional.

## Addendum: measured in the app (2026-10-27)

- `zerogpu-aoti/wan2-2-fp8da-aoti-faster` called from Node with `@gradio/client` and no token: a 3.5 s image-to-video clip returned a valid 832×480 MP4 in **61 s**.
- A follow-up 5 s request was refused: *"You have exceeded your ZeroGPU quota (150s requested vs. 126s left). Try again in 23:53:40."* So a **5 s clip reserves 150 s of quota**, about twice the ~70 s estimated above. Anonymous calls get roughly one short clip per day per server IP; a free-account token (5 min/day) gets about **2 five-second clips per day**. The quota resets 24 h after first use.
- The app now has this Space as its `huggingface` video provider. Quota errors refund the credits and show the reset time.
